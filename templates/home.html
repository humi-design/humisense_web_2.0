{# ------------------------------------------------------------------- 03 — HOW A WORKFLOW ACTUALLY RUNS ------------------------------------------------------------------- #}

Inside the system

What “AI automation” actually means here.

Not a chatbot bolted onto a form. A pipeline that reads incoming work, applies your rules, routes it, and hands anything uncertain to a person with the context to decide.

The steps below are the same shape whether the input is an RFQ, a supplier invoice, a purchase order or a support request.

AI automation services

01

Intake

Email, PDF, spreadsheet or portal submission arrives and is read into structured fields — including scanned documents and inconsistent formats.

02

Interpret

Line items, quantities, specifications and entities are matched against your catalogue, pricing rules and customer records.

03

Decide

Deterministic rules handle the routine majority. Where confidence is low, the item is flagged rather than guessed at.

04

Act

A quotation is drafted, a record is written to your ERP or CRM, a supplier is contacted, or a task is created for the right person.

05

Review

Every step is logged with its inputs and confidence. Anything the system was unsure about waits in a review queue with the original document attached.

Where it applies

Built for operations that run on documents.

The pattern is the same across sectors: high volumes of semi-structured input, a decision that follows rules, and a person who currently does that work by hand.

Manufacturing

RFQs, supplier correspondence, purchase orders, quality documentation and production reporting.

Industrial distribution

Customer RFQs, multi-supplier comparison, quotation preparation, product matching and follow-up.

Wholesale & B2B

Lead qualification, enquiry handling, quotation management and order administration.

Professional services

Document intake, engagement drafting, reporting and client communication at volume.

Small businesses

One or two workflows automated properly, rather than a platform nobody has time to run.

B2B businesses

Sales pipeline automation, lead routing and the paperwork between enquiry and order.
{# ------------------------------------------------------------------- 05 — HOW WE WORK (five stages) ------------------------------------------------------------------- #}

How we work

From possibility to reality.

Five stages. The first two decide whether the project is worth building at all, which is why we do not skip them.

Discover

Understand the process, measure the current baseline and identify where automation actually pays back.

Design

Choose the architecture and the experience. Rules where rules suffice, models only where judgement is needed.

Build

Engineer the system, integrate it with your existing tools and test it against real inputs, not samples.

Deploy

Ship behind a review path, monitor the first weeks and tune the thresholds against actual output.

Evolve

Improve and extend. Handover includes the logging and review paths needed to run it without us.
