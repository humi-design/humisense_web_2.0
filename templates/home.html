{# =================================================================== PROCESS =================================================================== #}

How we work

From possibility to reality.

Four stages. The first two decide whether the project is worth building at all, which is why we do not skip them.

01

Discover

Understand the problem, measure the current process and identify where automation actually pays back.

02

Design

Choose the architecture and the experience. Rules where rules suffice, models only where judgement is needed.

03

Build

Engineer the product or automation, integrate it with your systems and test it against real inputs.

04

Evolve

Monitor, improve and scale. Handover includes the logging and review paths needed to run it without us.
