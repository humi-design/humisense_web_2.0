Our Mission

Make AI useful in real operations

Most businesses do not need another AI demonstration. They need the repetitive document and coordination work around their core systems to stop consuming skilled people's time. That is the work we take on.

We are a technology implementation partner: we map the process, build the automation against your existing systems, and hand it over with documentation, monitoring and a defined human review point.

Our Vision

Automation that survives production

A pilot that works in a demo and fails on real volume has no value. Our standard is a system that runs on your actual documents and enquiries, with logging, confidence thresholds and an exception path.

That means being honest about limits. AI reads, checks and routes. It does not approve substitutions, release deviations or make commitments on your behalf.

Why We Exist

The HUMISENSE Story

HUMISENSE started from a narrow observation: the work that slows a business down is rarely the work it considers strategic. It is the quotation that has to be rebuilt from a PDF, the purchase order keyed in twice, the supplier email thread nobody owns.

That work is repetitive, it is measurable, and most of it can be handled by a system that reads, checks and routes — provided someone builds the check and the exception path properly. That is the part we take seriously.

Today we work with manufacturing, distribution, wholesale and professional services businesses internationally, delivering AI automation in workflow, document processing, RFQ handling and AI agent development. Our Academy shares the same practical approach through hands-on courses.

How we work

Four things we do not compromise on

These are working rules, not slogans. They decide what we accept and what we decline.

01

No demo-only systems

If a pipeline cannot run on your real documents at your real volume, it is not finished. We test against your data before calling anything delivered.

02

A human point stays in the loop

Every pipeline we hand over has a defined review step and an exception path. Automation reads, checks and routes. It does not commit on your behalf.

03

Measured or not claimed

We will not tell you a system improves accuracy without a baseline to compare against. If we cannot measure it, we say so and propose how we would.

04

Documented handover

You get the architecture, the prompts, the evaluation set and the runbook. A system only your supplier can maintain is not an asset.
